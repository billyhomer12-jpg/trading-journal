Frequently asked questions

Your data

Where is my journal stored?

On your device only. Trades, balances, accounts, payouts, notes and imported screenshots are saved in the App's local storage and are not uploaded to us, even when you're signed in. Your account (email, username, photo, preferences) is stored separately with our authentication provider. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my journal or move it to a new phone?

Open Settings → Data and tap Export JSON to save a backup file. On the new device, open Settings → Data and choose that file under Import JSON file. Because data is local, uninstalling the App or clearing browser data without a backup deletes it permanently.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Not now on the sign-in screen to use the journal without one. An account adds a username, profile photo and two-factor security, and it will be needed for future features such as sync.

Accounts, balances and payouts

Why do balances only change through imports?

So your numbers always match your firm. Use Import on the Accounts tab: attach a screenshot of your firm's dashboard (it dates the import and is kept for reference), enter or paste the balances, and check the preview of every change before anything is saved.

How are trailing drawdown and "cushion" calculated?

The App estimates each account's trailing stop from its highest recorded balance and the drawdown buffer you set for that firm. Cushion is how far your balance is above that stop. These are estimates, and every firm applies its rules in its own way, so always confirm with your firm's dashboard. See the Disclaimer.

Do recorded payouts change my balance?

No. Payouts are a log of withdrawals and totals. Balances change only through imports, which reflect what your firm reports after a payout.

Is this app connected to Take Profit Trader, Tradeify or Lucid?

No. Trading Journal is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any prop firm or by Forex Factory. It doesn't connect to your firm or broker and can't place trades.

News

The News tab says "Couldn't refresh". Why?

The economic calendar comes from a public Forex Factory feed that limits how often it can be requested. When a refresh fails, the App shows the last copy it downloaded. Try again later, or check forexfactory.com/calendar directly.

Sign-in and security

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I turn on two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Go to Settings → Security → Authenticator app, scan the QR code with an app such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password, and enter the 6-digit code. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe.

I lost my phone with my authenticator app.

Sign in and use one of your recovery codes instead of the 6-digit code. Each code works once. Then turn 2FA off and on again in Settings to set up your new device. If you've lost your recovery codes too, email us from your account's email address.

How do I change my username, photo or marketing emails?

Settings → Profile. Marketing emails are off unless you turned them on, and you can switch them off there at any time, or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not in the App. iPhone/iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open the App and tap Restore purchases in Settings.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: request a refund from your order history in the Play Store.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Profile → Delete account. Full details, including how to request deletion by email, are on the account deletion page.

Can I use it on the web or a computer?

Yes. Open the web app in Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android and desktop Chrome, use Install app. Your journal on each device is separate unless you export and restore it.
