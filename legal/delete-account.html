Delete your Trading Journal account

App: Trading Journal · Developer: Billy Homer · Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can delete your Trading Journal account, and the data linked to it, at any time. You don't need to contact us if you can open the App.

Option 1: Delete in the App (immediate)

  1. Open Trading Journal and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⚙ Settings button (or your profile photo in the header).
  3. Open Profile and scroll down to Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password. If two-factor authentication is on, you'll also need a code from your authenticator app or a recovery code.
  5. Optionally, tick erase my journal on this device to also remove the journal stored on that device.
  6. Tap Delete account. You'll be signed out, and your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't sign in or no longer have the App, email billyhomer12@gmail.com

  • Send the email from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Trading Journal account", and include your username if you remember it.
  • We may ask you to confirm the request to make sure it's really you.
  • We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address, username and password hash
  • Your profile photo
  • Your theme preference and marketing-email choice (including opt-in/opt-out timestamps)
  • Your two-factor authentication settings and recovery codes

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Backups: deleted data can stay in encrypted infrastructure backups for up to 30 days, then it is overwritten.
  • Security logs (such as sign-in attempts and IP addresses kept by our hosting providers): up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: if you bought a subscription, Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep transaction records under their own policies, and we keep what tax and accounting laws require.
  • Support emails you sent us: up to 24 months.

Your journal data

Trades, balances, accounts, payouts, notes and screenshots are stored only on your device, never on our servers, so we can't delete them for you remotely. To remove them, tick "erase my journal on this device" when you delete your account, use Settings → Clear all data, uninstall the App, or clear the site data in your browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel first:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Trading Journal → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Trading Journal → Cancel.
