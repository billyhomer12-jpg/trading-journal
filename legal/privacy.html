3. Data that stays on your device

Your journal content (prop-firm accounts, balances, trades and P&L, journal notes, behavior tags, payouts, settings, and any screenshots you import) is stored only on your device, in the App's local storage. It is not uploaded to us or to Supabase and is not linked to your account.

  • Because we don't have a copy, we can't recover it for you. Use Settings → Data → Export JSON to keep your own backup.
  • Uninstalling the App, clearing your browser's site data, or choosing to erase the journal removes it from that device.
  • If we add cloud sync or backup in the future, it will be optional, we will update this policy first, and we will ask before any journal data leaves your device.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We show no ads and include no advertising SDKs.
  • We use no analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party tracking cookies, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use your data to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

5. How we use information

  • To create and run your account, sign you in, and keep it secure (including 2FA and abuse prevention).
  • To send transactional emails, such as confirming your email address, password resets and important notices about the service or these policies.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you opted in. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe option, and you can switch them off in Settings → Profile → Marketing emails.
  • To provide and manage paid subscriptions once they're offered.
  • To answer support requests, fix bugs and improve the App.
  • To comply with the law, enforce our Terms of Use, and protect our rights and the rights of others.

6. Network requests and third-party content

  • Economic news. The News tab downloads a public economic-calendar feed (published for Forex Factory at nfs.faireconomy.media). As with any web request, that server can see your IP address and device/browser type. No account or journal information is sent. We are not affiliated with Forex Factory.
  • Code library. When accounts are enabled, the web version loads Supabase's open-source JavaScript library from a public content-delivery network (esm.sh), which can likewise see your IP address.
  • Hosting. The web version and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.
  • Links. The App links to prop-firm websites and help centers and to Forex Factory. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data (email, username, password hash, profile photo, preferences, marketing choice, 2FA data) is kept while your account exists. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Backups kept by our infrastructure provider can hold deleted data for a limited time, normally no more than 30 days, before it is overwritten.
  • Security logs are kept for a short period (typically up to 90 days) unless needed to investigate abuse.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their policies, and by us as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.
  • Journal data on your device stays there until you remove it. We never have a copy.

9. Deleting your account

In the App, go to Settings → Profile → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, profile, profile photo, 2FA settings and recovery codes. If you can't access the App, email billyhomer12@gmail.com from the address on your account. Full instructions are on the account deletion page. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your store account settings.

14. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords and recovery codes, row-level database access rules so each user can reach only their own records, and optional two-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and turn on 2FA. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

15. Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Children under 13 are not allowed to use it, and our Terms require users to be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.
